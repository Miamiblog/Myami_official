Foundation

One company. Multiple expressions.

MYAMI is designed as an ecosystem, not a collection of unrelated services.

The fundamental architecture of MYAMI is simple: one parent brand, multiple specialized divisions, shared infrastructure, and a common customer ecosystem.

Each division has its own commercial function, but none exists independently. Technology creates digital infrastructure. Media creates visibility and narrative. Entertainment creates physical experiences and cultural access. Wear converts brand recognition into physical identity.

01 · Infrastructure
MYAMI Tech

Builds the digital systems, websites, software and technology that brands operate on.

02 · Voice
MYAMI Media

Builds identity, content, storytelling and digital visibility.

03 · Experience
MYAMI Entertainment

Creates events, cultural projects, experiences and communities.

04 · Identity
MYAMI Wear

Turns the MYAMI worldview into physical products, fashion and lifestyle identity.

"Different businesses. One worldview."

Business Model

How MYAMI creates value.

MYAMI creates value by solving a recurring problem: ambitious people and organizations often have the vision, product or talent, but lack the infrastructure required to present it professionally and scale it.

MYAMI provides that infrastructure through a combination of technology, creative execution, cultural access and physical identity.

01 · ACQUIRE
Find ambitious clients
Through referrals, direct outreach, social media, partnerships, events and the MYAMI network.
New Opportunity
02 · DIAGNOSE
Understand the gap
Identify what the client has, what they lack, and where MYAMI can create measurable value.
Strategy
03 · BUILD
Deliver the solution
Deploy the appropriate combination of technology, branding, content, events or physical identity.
Client Value
04 · RETAIN
Convert projects into relationships
Maintenance, content retainers, strategy, recurring campaigns and long-term partnerships.
Recurring Revenue
05 · EXPAND
Move across divisions

Customer Lifetime Value
MYAMI Ecosystem

The MYAMI flywheel.

The long-term advantage of MYAMI is not any individual service. It is the relationship between the divisions.

Each division can generate customers, attention, revenue and opportunities for the others. Over time this creates an internal flywheel where every successful project increases the potential value of the entire company.

01
Tech

Creates the first commercial relationship and digital infrastructure.

02
Media

Increases visibility, narrative and audience around the client.

03
Entertainment

Converts attention into experiences, communities and cultural participation.

04
Wear

Turns recognition and community into physical products and brand equity.

The objective is not simply to sell more services. It is to make every relationship more valuable over time.

Customer Journey

From first contact to long-term partner.

MYAMI's customer experience should feel intentional from the first interaction. The objective is to move customers away from viewing MYAMI as a vendor and toward viewing MYAMI as an infrastructure partner.

01
Discovery
The customer encounters MYAMI through a referral, social content, event, portfolio, website or direct outreach.
02
Conversation
MYAMI identifies the customer's current position, objectives, challenges and opportunities.
03
Proposal
A clear solution is presented with defined scope, deliverables, timeline and commercial terms.
04
Execution
MYAMI builds, creates or executes the agreed solution while documenting the process and results.
05
Delivery
The customer receives a finished product and an experience that reinforces MYAMI's premium positioning.
06
Expansion
Where relevant, the relationship expands into additional services, retainers, collaborations or other divisions.
Revenue Engine

Multiple revenue layers.

MYAMI is intentionally designed to avoid dependence on a single revenue source. The business begins with service revenue because it requires relatively low capital to start, then progressively introduces recurring and scalable revenue.

Layer 01
Project Revenue

Websites, branding, content packages, event campaigns, digital systems and other defined deliverables.

Layer 02
Recurring Revenue

Maintenance, content retainers, social management, strategy and ongoing creative support.

Layer 03
Experience Revenue

Events, partnerships, activations, ticketing, sponsorship and cultural projects.

Layer 04
Product Revenue

MYAMI Wear, merchandise, limited drops and collaboration products.

Layer 05
Partnership Revenue

Strategic partnerships, collaborations, co-branded initiatives and sponsorships.

Layer 06
Intellectual Property

Future opportunities built around MYAMI's audience, systems, creative assets and proprietary products.

Competitive Advantage

Why MYAMI can win.

MYAMI does not need to become the biggest company in the market to become valuable. It needs to become exceptionally good at a specific intersection: technology + creative execution + culture.

Integrated capability

Clients can access technology, branding, media and experiences through one ecosystem instead of coordinating multiple disconnected providers.

Founder-led execution

In the foundation phase, the company remains close to the work. This allows fast decisions, low overhead and strong quality control.

Cultural proximity

MYAMI is built from within the market it intends to serve, giving it direct understanding of youth culture, creators, campus communities and emerging brands.

Brand-first architecture

MYAMI is building the company identity and systems before attempting aggressive scale. Recognition itself becomes a long-term asset.

Cross-selling potential

A single client relationship can generate multiple commercial opportunities across divisions.

Low-capital starting point

Service businesses allow MYAMI to generate early revenue before capital-intensive divisions such as Wear and large-scale Entertainment are fully activated.

Market Position

The space MYAMI intends to own.

Not the cheapest. Not the loudest. The most intentional.

MYAMI is positioned between traditional creative agencies, freelance service providers, technology studios and entertainment brands.

The company is intentionally positioned as a premium, culturally aware, digitally capable creative company rather than a generic agency.

Against Commodity
Not a Freelancer Marketplace

MYAMI competes on identity, systems, quality and long-term relationships rather than the lowest price.

Against Traditional
Not a Conventional Agency

MYAMI is more flexible, digitally native and culturally connected than traditional agency structures.

For the Market
Infrastructure for Ambition

MYAMI exists for people and brands whose ambition has outgrown their current infrastructure.

Execution Priorities

What gets built first.

MYAMI's biggest risk at the foundation stage is not lack of opportunity. It is attempting to activate everything at once. The business therefore follows a deliberate sequence.

01
Strengthen Tech. Build a reliable pipeline of web development, software and digital-system clients.
02
Build Media. Establish MYAMI's creative reputation through content, branding and documented work.
03
Build the portfolio. Every meaningful project becomes proof, case study, content and future sales material.
04
Build recurring revenue. Move suitable clients from one-off projects into retainers and long-term relationships.
05
Activate Entertainment. Use existing cultural access to build events, partnerships and community.
06
Seed Wear. Introduce physical products only after sufficient brand recognition and community have been established.
07
Scale the team. Replace founder dependency with systems, specialists, collaborators and eventually division leads.
Team Structure

From founder-led to company-led.

MYAMI begins with a highly centralized founder-led structure. This is intentional. At the foundation stage, speed, quality control and capital efficiency matter more than organizational complexity.

As revenue grows, the structure evolves from one person executing multiple functions into a company where each division has dedicated responsibility.

01
Founder / CEO

Vision, strategy, brand direction, major relationships, business development and final decision-making.

02
Division Leads

Responsible for the commercial and creative performance of Tech, Media, Entertainment and Wear.

03
Creative Specialists

Developers, designers, photographers, editors, writers and other project-specific specialists.

04
Operations

Finance, documentation, project management, administration and internal systems.

05
Partnership Network

External collaborators, venues, brands, creators, vendors and strategic partners.

06
MYAMI Talent

Future talent infrastructure connecting selected creators and personalities to opportunities.

Operating Principles

The company runs on standards.

Strategy only matters when it becomes behavior. These principles translate the MYAMI identity into operational decisions.

01
Build Before Scaling

Establish repeatable systems and proof before adding unnecessary complexity.

02
Revenue Before Vanity

Attention is useful, but sustainable revenue is what turns attention into a company.

03
Document Everything

Projects, processes, results, failures and lessons become institutional knowledge and marketing assets.

04
Protect the Brand

Not every opportunity is a good opportunity. MYAMI chooses work that strengthens its position.

05
Quality Compounds

Every project should make the next project easier to win because the body of proof keeps getting stronger.

06
Think Long-Term

The objective is not simply to make money this month. It is to build an asset that becomes increasingly valuable.

"The business is being built to outlive the founder's daily involvement."
